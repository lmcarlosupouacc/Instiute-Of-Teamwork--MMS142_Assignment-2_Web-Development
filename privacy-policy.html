Memasuot Privacy Policy

Date of Implementation: October 15, 2026

Introduction

Welcome to Memasuot! Your privacy and the protection of your personal information are important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, process, and protect your personal information whenever you visit, browse, register, or make purchases through our e-commerce website.

By using our website and its services, you acknowledge that you have read and understood this Privacy Policy. We are committed to handling your personal information responsibly and in accordance with applicable data protection laws, including the Philippine Data Privacy Act of 2012 (Republic Act No. 10173).

This policy applies to all customers, registered users, and visitors who interact with our website, including those who purchase products, contact customer support, or subscribe to promotional updates.

What Data Do We Collect From You?

When you visit or make purchases through Memasuot, we may collect different types of personal information to provide you with a convenient and efficient shopping experience.

Personal Information
• Full name
• Email address
• Contact number
• Billing and shipping address

Account Information
• Username and account credentials
• Password and login information
• Account preferences and saved addresses

Transaction and Payment Information
• Products purchased
• Order history and transaction records
• Payment method and payment status
• Billing details and refund information

We do not necessarily store complete payment card details. Sensitive payment information may instead be processed directly by authorized payment service providers.

Technical and Website Information
When you access our website, we may automatically collect certain technical information, including your IP address, browser type, device information, operating system, pages visited, and browsing activity.

Our Purpose For Collecting Them

We collect personal information for legitimate business purposes and to ensure that our customers receive reliable services when shopping through our website:

Processing Orders and Purchases: We use your personal information to confirm purchases, process transactions, prepare orders, and ensure that products are delivered to the correct address.

Managing Customer Accounts: Your information allows us to create and maintain customer accounts, verify account ownership, and provide access to account-related features.

Payment Verification and Security: We use transaction-related information to confirm payments, detect suspicious activities, prevent fraudulent transactions, and maintain secure purchasing processes.

Customer Support and Communication: Your contact information allows us to respond to inquiries, address complaints, provide order updates, and assist with concerns regarding products or services.

Marketing and Promotions: With your appropriate consent, we may use your email address or contact information to send promotional announcements, special discounts, product recommendations, and updates about upcoming sales.

Website Improvement: We may analyze website activity and customer preferences to improve our website design, product selection, navigation, and overall shopping experience.

Our Children's Data Clause

Memasuot recognizes the importance of protecting the privacy of children and minors who may access online services. Our website is not intended to independently collect personal information from children without appropriate parental or legal guardian involvement and consent where required.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children without the necessary authorization. If we discover that personal information has been collected from a child without appropriate consent, we will take reasonable steps to review the situation and delete or restrict the information when appropriate and legally permitted.

How Do We Protect Your Data?

We take reasonable organizational, physical, and technical measures to protect the personal information entrusted to us, including HTTPS secure website connections, account protection, restricted personnel access, trusted third-party payment processing, and ongoing security monitoring.

What Are Your Rights?

As a customer or user of Memasuot, you have rights concerning your personal information under the Philippine Data Privacy Act of 2012, including the Right to Be Informed, Right to Access, Right to Rectification, Right to Erasure or Blocking, Right to Object, Right to Withdraw Consent, and Right to File a Complaint.

About Third Parties

To operate our e-commerce website efficiently, we may work with selected third-party service providers including payment service providers (GCash, Maya, banks), delivery and courier services, website hosting providers, and analytics services. We do not sell customers' personal information to third parties for their independent commercial use.

How Long Do We Keep Your Data?

Memasuot retains personal information only for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes for which it was collected, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, and support legitimate business operations.

Cookies And Other Trackers

Our website may use essential, preference, analytics, and marketing cookies to improve website functionality and provide customers with a more personalized shopping experience. You may manage or disable cookies through your browser settings.
